Eleos was the first star-men and King of the Agustonian independent island of Arad-Astrim for most part of his life; He was a local monarch who conquered the respect of many Lutennos from mainland Agustonia, but wasn't named by the Emperors as one of them; Eleos was also a general and strategist on military affairs, a paladin of Tor, and astronomical mathematician and cartographer. His children and grandchildren followed the royal lineage of star-men after his death, with the Lux Nul (Black Star) as their Birthmark in the back of their necks.

During his entire life, the King - and his elite knights - trained more than fifteen thousand soldiers in his island that were sent to the Imperial Guard, and fought more than forty battles against enemies of the empire at sea and at coast. He was the greatest cartographer the [[Empire]] ever had, decorating his entire palace with a variety of maps in leather parchments. Some call him the 'Black Blood King' for the origin of his family and the Black Star, but most Lutennos liked and gifted him for his fatherly kindness and philosophy of the Astriminis. Some soldiers that were trained by him believed that he took care of every single man that stepped in the island as if they were his children, a fatherly King, some said. But in Agustonian tradition, he was seen as a man who enjoyed difficulty and stress, and melodies sing about 'barefoot King Eleos stepping on pointy pebbles to meet his maiden, as he never reached there, for the long ways he chose to follow.'. Eleos believed that a man could only become wiser after suffering enough to step forth, feel hunger to enjoy food, feel hurt to know pain, feel betrayed to know the value of trust, and so it goes to every human aspects. Eleos believes that adversity can produce understanding. 

The island of Arad-Astrim reflected his philosophy, because it has, to this day, extreme environmental conditions to the average life, making it difficult to breathe, harvest and live.  The Astrimini House lived there since the beginning of their story, and people from the continent who had been there could never understand how could they live in such a place, 'with nothing but black rocks'.

### The Black Star (Lux Nul)

It is a birthmark symbol given by the Minor Deity Aggoreos once his oath was said and his appliance ritual was performed by one. It is unknown who did the ritual to be given the star, but it has remained to every single person of the Astrimini House. Perhaps some old grandfather of Eleos, or great grandfather, but the reason still remains unknown.

*"Loyance from afar, men who yearn for love in a wretched world of doom. Vouchsafe, O shining one, thy seed unto us."*

Aggoreos was a mysterious deity who tempted common men to slouching and comfort because it was a form of feeding his existence. As if cheap pleasure made by men fed his whole. In a forgotten time, some miserable men became puppets of the deity for their own choice, honoring sigils and burning birthmarks in body spots of their will. It is said that the first ones who honored the deity wanted comfort from it, and so they had received, devoting their soul and emptying themselves to feed the creature, becoming hollow vessels of insatiable pleasures. No one knew the names of those, nor where they had come from, their final days, nothing. It is only known that the birthmarks had stood with them and their children, and their future generations, forever. King Eleos was just one with the Dark Star, one of the symbols, that was also passed down to his children, and, with fear of the curse this deity has given them, Eleos taught his children to dodge slouchiness and cheap pleasures, strenghtening his philosohphy. Either way, some of his sons and daughters, grandsons and granddaughters dislike this harshness of their father, which made their capacity to feel love or pleasures harder to achieve, making them sentimentally apart.
