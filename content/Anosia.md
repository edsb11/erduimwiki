Anosia is, briefly, the continent where most of the living beings in Erduim reside. It is a massive, vast land full of diferent species, biomes, and natural formations that no other world had seen. It is considered the main continent for the world's history, mainly after the Gods and Deities had arrived Erduim and settled in the continent.

# Locations:

---
### Rinnia peninsula, central Anosia:

The greatest region conquered and ruled by an empire. Pretty much all of the peninsula is governed by the [[Empire of Agustonia]]. The peninsula is known for it's far wide yellow-ish fields in the south contrasting with the mountain range in the east, and the wide beaches in the edges of the terrain. Powerful and charming forts are built in several beaches around the peninsula's perimeter, as well as cathedrals and religious monuments in the big cities. The region is very important to the Gods' history in Erduim, and Humans too, considered to be the center of the entire world's history. A cloudy and cold region at the south, but sunny and warm at the north.
In the west coast, there are unusual forests with gigantic trees and thick mana fog, believed to be where the last source of energy the gods had left to all species. In the near northern seaside, there is the notorious Gods' Archipelago, where it is not considered to be part of a greater empire or state, but rather a historical place with a few villages. Some islands are really close to the Rinnia peninsula, where they can be seen in the horizon by the coasts.
