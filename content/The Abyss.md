"To the first glimpse of breath and sight, mankind had the flash of the abyss. Far from every other world they could ever be. Not only from men, but far from any kind of life in every dimension."

The Abyss is the first dimension to ever exist, where the absolute creator [[Ulm]] resides. With unimaginable distance to come across just to reach the surface edge of it. The dimension is known for its pitch black space, so dark that the average eye is cursed forever and never able to absorb light again. A medium abscent in life, but filled with matter, star powder, atoms and molecules.

It is a dead space with no land to be stepped on and no air to be breathed. And from the beginning of the universe's first moments, its dark matter expands infinitely, with no sign of having a known limit. Until this day, the abyss grows and surrounds all of the other dimensions, being the last of the dimensional layers. 

Even though the Abyss was not created by any god, Ulm rules it all. They have the power of creation, but they used it in the lower dimensions under (and inside) the Abyss.

Most of the dimension's history is told at the genesis of the universe and the growing life of Ulm, being an empty space with not much to be said.

## Regions of the Abyss

Although continents and islands are not seen in this dimension, there are some regions seen by the living eyes.

### The Cradle of Ulm
A region where a star resides nowadays. It's where most of space matter is.

### The 'Iron' Ring
The ring of orbiting rocks and other matter around the Ethereal plain
