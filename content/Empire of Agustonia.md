Agustonia is a great empire located at the Rinnia Peninsula, near the central regions of [[Anosia]]. It is reigned by four crowns (or four emperors), who rule each region based on the four directions of the globe (currently, the emperors are the only ones who reign, not anymore serving the Clidrios). It extends its cultural borders to almost every other kingdom in the continent, and many of the city states' governments are inspired by the civillian code once created and used by the Agustonian emperors. The southern borders, where the peninsula is covered by continental land, are neighbors with the Reonwanni Mountain Chain and the Golden Fields (Galladia). After the Galladian frontiers in the south, it is considered no more as a sovereign territory of the empire. Overseas, the empire is also great in its maritime market, trading with almost every other coastal kingdom. There are high strandard living in the great cities like Burg La'Ravnnia, where merchants lead the economy with an immense variety of goods, from armor plates to wine. The great cities have a very predominant use of Ash granite, rough and resistant material coming from ancient creatures' bones mixed with other dust, making the city with a grey-ish/yellow-ish color palette. Far from the great Burgs there are smaller towns and villages, built with cobblestone by the farmers and field workers themselves. Rural lifestyle differs, because many villages work and deliver their products to the local castle and local Dux, while the Lord delivers it to the capital cities. In a change, the Dux offers protection with his knights and benefits to the town.

The history of the empire is classified by three grand periods, each one divided by the reigning Clidrios of that period. The first one, the *Ixpansia* (Expansion), known for the great crusades of expansion performed by the Duxes and Lutennos and the Clidrios itself. The second one, the *Fiirnos Audalla* (The Burnt Surface World), known for the great Agustonian wars and the tyranny reigning of the Second Clidrios, which enforced mass slavery, several prohibitions and crises, sieges and loots, and race division inside the lands of the empire. Finally, after the death of the Second Clidrios during the Campannia Ergo Sudivocca (Campaign against the people of the south), the Agustonian costumes were reestablished. People were uprising their own cities over a change since the Second Clidrios' death, and the greatest insurrection events have happened ever since. The rulers redefined the empire structure entirely, changing everything from what it was. The third period and current one kept order and freedom at a balance, shrinking the empire to an amount of capitals and cities that the government could manage without breaking its order, like it was in the past. The four emperors rule without the management of a Clidrios, and they are the messangers of Tor themselves instead of only one figure.

### Political structure

For long enough the Clidrios has been the highest role of all the empire. They were nominated by Tor itself, serving under the god as a messenger to the people of Erduim, being the only one who had direct contact with Tor and the Hara. Under the Clidrios (and during the first two eras of the empire), there were the Four Emperors, administrators of their regions in the peninsula based off of the four directions of a compass, interdependently projecting laws based off of the needs for their regions and cities. Yearly, the emperors reunite in the Palace of Mitra, at Burg La'Ravnnia, to discuss regional affairs, conflicts and perspectives, with the Clidrios sitting above them, as the evaluator of their discussions. The emperors are official kings of the four great capitals of the empire. The emperors and the Clidrios have their Ort la Llempus als Chevaliers Ampiria (The Corageous Order of Knights of the Empire), an official guard of elite armored knights bathed with blessings from Paladins and Tor itself.

After the fall of the Second Clidrios, the Emperors shrunk the political power that the Clidrios could enforce, and through divine summoning they could have a speech with Tor and message him to revoke some of the Clidrios' magical powers. And so it happened. The Clidrios was maintained as a traditional figure of royalty, but the ones who truly rule in the Third Age are the Four Emperors. This event lead to internal political fights against the people who thought this act of reducing the Clidrios was illegitimate. Those are called the Hidallios, a great religious and political force of conservative thinkers who want the Clidrios to have his magical and political powers concealed back, not only because his significance was sacred, but because the Clidrios itself blessed almost every land in the Empire, so reducing him has reduced the blessings concealed to the common people. Their hate for the emperors lead to sabotaging some of the political affairs and organizations. 

City governors and kings are the Lutennos, those who are chosen by the Emperors to lead castles and monasteries around the empire. They often live in royal families in smaller towns or Burgs. Lutennos manage the city with bureaucracy while the hard work goes to the peasants and land workers, and some of those papers are sent to the emperors so they can discuss their changes. Messages are always exchanged between all the cities with the four emperors, and sometimes even the rapture and fraud of those messages are often seen in the dirt roads, performed by expert rogues to ask the emperors for resources or changes in a place of their wish.

Lastly, as lesser leaders, there are the Duxes, or singluarly, the Dux. Those are often military leaders, wealthy noblemen or socially appraised people who are the head of small villages and towns, but still they do have political power and are forced by the Lutennos to exchange messages with the empire.

The empire streets are always in constant movement, where Distros, knights of the long street, scout every day and knight to maintain order along any empty place.

### Political strucutre and Magic

Since the beginning of the empire Tor had blessed the humans with benefits, but those are varied depending on the needs of that specific townsfolk. It is often observed the formation of Clerics and Paladins who are specialized in combat magic and magic protection. Magic usage is also prohibited in the capitals, and universities are isolated and severely secured by the Emperor's guard. The Clidrios can manipulate divine energy at their will and spend this energy to cast powerful divinations such as buffs, protections, channelings with Tor and smitings against Erduim creatures and people. The second Clidrios is a great example of combat magic usage, for the amount of blood spilled by only one man. With the greatest spells in range and power and post effects after hit, most pure energy that can pierce through any average body, no matter the protection casted upon, the Clidrios can be a dangerous figure in this world if not disciplined.


So, summarizing the power structure
Tor>Clidrios>Emperors>Lutennos>Dux

### Economy

The economy has been unstable for most part of the Agustonian history, with high amounts of stealing hands waiting for travelers on the lonely streets, or pirates wandering in the near coast, stealing and vanquishing commercial ships. Stabilization could only be achieved after the reestablishment proposed by the Third Clidrios.

Currently, the economy relies on the growth of urban Burgs and capitals through extraction and exploration. In the inner continent, great farmlands are often paid by near cities Lutennos to deliver part of that harvest to the city markets. Farmers and working boys come to the cities in times of harvest to trade and sell food and goods, filling the city with joy when the time comes. Trading guilds are organized by sophisticated sailors to sell and purchase imported goods from other kingdoms and countries. Low import duties are enforced to foreign sailors because of the peninsula extension, making it easier for coastal Agustonian ports to be filled with foreign boats and ships, encouraging them to create small port bay economies. Otherwise, the price of import duties are flexible to the value and quality of the transporting good, with taxes to luxury commodities higher than basic goods. A high amount of the coastal economy is maintained in maritime secutiry, the Marinnos are sea knights who protect the bay trades from pirates.

Lutennos are great managers of their local economy too. They are paid a small quantity by the capitals to sustain themselves with their production and to send a part of it to the big cities when the stock is enough. Coin is also used to sustain structures and maintain the town quality, fixing roads, storaging grains, military protection, and legal protection over land property. Food administration is also one of the most important economic pillars, Lutennos are to distribute food and storage the amounts needed so the people won't starve. Granaries are one of the most important foundations of a town, and their administration and protection are invested by the Lutennos. The high traders have their own section in the castle of the Lutenno, with maps and stocking provisions to prepare themselves in their voyages. Groups of farmworkers, trades from other cities, and small innhouses conjoin to rise the local economies. Of course, there are other forms to make the cogwheel of coin spin, but the main ones were explained in this document. Metalworkers and mining companies are also seen elsewhere, transforming raw materials into valuable manufactured goods or building refined materials, sending them to shipbuilding, weaponry manufacturing, city construction, and specialized craftsmanship.

A central treasury is kept in Burg La'Ravnnia to sustain the country in periods of crisis, with a high amount secured by taxes upon some economic institutions. There are flexible economic retractions during countryside winters, where communication and transportation of goods between farms and cities are harder. During wars, diplomatic missions, emergencies and major infrastructure planning there is also use of this treasury. The usage of this coin is also a conflict between the people and the Emperors, because when the coin is applied to attenuate or aggravate the situation, most people consider it not enough or that the emperors dislike spending the treasury. 

One of the declining forms of raising the purse of coin are the Genuvia, or simply the imperial crusades. Orders of knights, squires and Distros leave the country, often near southern Galladia, to loot outer villages and assimilate the people into Tor's blessings and beliefs. This sector was predominant during the Second Age, where the empire expanded after its frontiers, and until these days, the cultural, economical, and political changes induced by Agustonia can be seen everywhere. The Genuvia still happens, but not with the same investment as done in the past. Each new reunion between the emperors make this institution weaker while making the maritime trade stronger. Hidallios are predominant in this sector, looting cities to sustain their local institution, replacing the investment from the government for their own conquering treasures. The Genuvia is kept by these knights who can be considered a rising power against the current Agustonian state, although not so strong as the Guard (The country guard).

### Geography

With its great size, the Rinnia peninsula has very unique natural formations. Each region of the empire also accomodate unique populations and groups of villages, while others are where the Burgs lay: Larger and more populous cities.  
Coming from the northwest, the stronger winds from the ocean pierce through the lands bringing the largest of clouds to the south, and cold air to the northeast. This keeps the empire with an equal temperature rate to several regions. 

#### Region of Galladia

Elevation: ~450m above sea level

Galladia, being one of the greatest and more prestigious region, paints its yellow fields and lakes across the southern lands, while grass elevations segregate the flowery fields from the plain grasslands. In the base of every mountain there are big rock formations. Cloudy skies are predominant, and the southern mountain chains, one of the country's frontier lines, is always visible, making it easier for the local folk to localize themselves with a compass. Wide open cobblestone roads and commercial lines connect villages and Burgs, making it easy for commoners to travel around the empire. 


#### Region of Roftori

Elevation: ~40m above sea level

Roftori is one of the first regions to be populated by the Imperials, long before the empire itself was consolidated. Even though there are no mountain chains like in the south, here there are singular jagged and rocky mountains, where many military forts reside, with large and wide pine tree forests below, after the mountain base. Black rock beaches line the coastline, presenting a unique appearance along the border of the continent and to those approaching land after a long time traveling by ship, also making it difficult to ships to simply land in the coast, rising the value of those ports. Clouds drifting from the south normally descend to land, often making every place foggy while the sky is clear, and it barely rains at Roftori. Coastal winds often bend trees due to their strenght, and quickly push away the fog from beaches. High rock formations lay in the coastal lines, looking like great rocky walls. At lower altitudes, you often see flowery valleys, rocky flat lakes and colorful trees, but it's a very rare seasonal occasion for the flora to bloom so colorfully. The archipelago of all gods is near the Roftori coast, bathed by the fog, but in a distance visible to the naked eye.
Geopolitically and symbolically, Roftori is the most important region. With great cities, monuments, buildings and systems. Humans lived in the region long before the Smite, working in small groups of farmers and fishermen. And when the gods were vanished, the vast majority of humans migrated from the archipelago to the mainland Rinnia peninsula. 
#### County of Lessocht

Elevation: ~20m above sea level

Lessocht is a small region in the north, known for its independent political power and local economic sustainability. There is a great lake connected to the ocean by a river which locals believe to be *where souls of all gods are sleeping after being smitten by Tor*. Despite it being a local legend, the lake holds a vast amount of natural mana energy for unknown reasons, creating beautiful, shining blue waves along its entire expanse. Surrounding the lake is a dense and lush forest, which shares the same pine tree forest characteristics found in Roftori.

#### Mount Solgur

It is believed that Mt. Solgur has been where scholars formulated, for the first time, the concept of compass directions. Four unique mountains are almost perfectly aligned at this region: One to the north, south, west and east. Symbollicaly representing the four directions of the globe, and the four emperors, who rule the empire under the sacred Clidrios. Amidst the mountains lies Gavelo Abbey (representing the wisdom and sacred knowledge of the Clidrios among the emperors), where scholars and mathematicians live covered by shadows and almost no access to sunlight. The trajectory of the Ekkasardi sun aligned perfectly with the top of the eastern and western mountains.

#### Runamarsch and Aussulem

Two regions which are often unified as one for where the greatest roads and commercial routes were built. Yellow Runa flowers cover all the green grass extensions throughout the region, and it is one of the calmest places to be in the continent. The sky is normally blue and clear, and many animals live in groups, feeding themselves with Runa, a very nutritious plant.

#### Island of Arad-Astrim

Island of the Star-men. An ancient strain of old kings which was founded by [[King Eleos]]' first ten children, all of them with star-shaped scars on their bellies, their mark. The island is constituted by black rock formations in its entirety, and small darkwood groves. In its center, resides the castletown of the Arad-Astrimmos, surrounded by people who serve the keep and the empire. The island has extreme conditions in summer, which is often a place and season where knights of the Clidrio's Guard are trained.

#### Region of Soderne

Soderne is a region of dark green glades with elevations and smaller mounts, with a great coast, a mountain chain, and the Aurenoran mountain chain at the extreme south, which is the extremes of the country's borders. Sudeuxean people are often seen in this region, called the 'fishmen' by foreigners and mocked by their vulgar and elegant dialect, originating from the original imperial language.
The region has a long coast covered by the Lasine Ampere Loches bay at the northern side, which also favors their beach-esque culture with seafood and sailing. Soderne is a region of many different cultural ascendancies, with many of the species that aren't humans living there with tolerance (Like orcs, elves and such, without prohibitions like other regions), and also seen as the region of bohemian villages, mocked by people from the traditional culture.
At the eastern borders near Aurenora there are is the Simmi Battos, the High River of the East, originating from the great mountain cascade that fills the base of the mountains with fog and river strains.
